Frequently Asked Questions

1. How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 4-7 business days across India. Express shipping takes 2-4 days. We also offer same-day delivery in Vadodara and Ahmedabad.

2. How can I track my order?

Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking link via Email and SMS. You can also visit our 'Track Order' page.

3. What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major Credit/Debit Cards, UPI (GPay, PhonePe), and Net Banking. Cash on Delivery (COD) is also available.

4. Can I return a personalized gift?

Personalized items are unique to you and cannot be returned unless they are damaged or defective. Please check our Return Policy for details.

5. Do you ship internationally?

Currently, we only ship within India.
