Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 18, 2026

The Maryam ("we", "us", or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website themaryam.in.

We use the information we collect to:

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction.
